# My Favorite Movie: Shrek 🐸

## Why I Like It
- It’s a hilarious and heartwarming story about **acceptance** and **friendship**.  
- The humor works for both kids and adults.  
- The soundtrack is iconic — especially *“All Star”* by Smash Mouth. 🎵  

### Favorite Characters
1. **Shrek** – The grumpy ogre with a kind heart 💚  
2. **Donkey** – Funny, loyal, and always talkative 🐴  
3. **Puss in Boots** – The cutest swordsman ever 😺  

> *“Better out than in, I always say!”* — Shrek  

**Overall Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
